Powerful Features for Professional Bug Reports

Everything you need to create detailed, actionable bug reports that developers love

🖱️

Click & Input Tracking

Automatically captures every button click and form entry with field labels, so developers know exactly what you interacted with. No more guessing which button was clicked.

⚠️

Error Detection

Instantly records JavaScript errors and form validation messages as they happen. No more "it just broke" reports - developers get the exact error details.

🌐

Network Monitoring

Tracks API calls and identifies slow or failed requests. Perfect for debugging backend issues and understanding what's happening behind the scenes.

📥

HTML Export

Download beautifully formatted reports with all steps, details, and visual elements. Ready to share with your team in seconds.

📋

Text Copy

One-click copy of a plain text version perfect for pasting into Jira, emails, Slack, or any bug tracking system.

📸

Screenshots

Optionally capture visual snapshots at each step to show exactly what the page looked like when the bug occurred.

🔄

Multi-Page Support

Continues recording seamlessly as you navigate between pages. No need to restart for complex workflows that span multiple pages.

🔒

Sensitive Data Masking

Automatically hides passwords and credit card numbers to keep your reports secure. Your privacy is our priority.

⚙️

Customizable Settings

Choose exactly what to record - clicks, inputs, errors, network, screenshots. Tailor it to your specific needs and workflow.
